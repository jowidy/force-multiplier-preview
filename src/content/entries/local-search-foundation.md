## The online setup needed a clear owner and operating area

The company's service area crossed a state line. Its Google profile needed settings that reflected that focus, and an unused profile record was causing confusion about the correct setup.

The client's team had already created and verified the main business profile. The next step was to work from that profile and put the agreed service area in place.

## We updated the profile and clarified responsibilities

We gained manager access and changed the service-area settings around the market the business intended to serve. We also clarified which verified profile belonged with the active advertising setup.

The client's team removed the unused, unverified duplicate. Force Multiplier handled the service-area update and coordination around the active profile.

## What was confirmed

The verified profile had updated service-area settings, and the client's team had removed the unused duplicate. The business had an identified account and an agreed operating area to use for the next marketing work.

That distinction in responsibility matters: the client established and verified the main profile, while we helped align and maintain its settings.

## The foundation for further work

This anonymized case documents account and service-area corrections. It does not establish a ranking improvement or additional inquiries.

The useful outcome was a clearer starting point: the correct profile, accurate operating information and a shared understanding of which account to maintain.
