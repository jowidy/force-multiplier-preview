Technology is part of how Force Multiplier works. In developing our own website and campaign plans, AI assistance has supported research, planning, wording options, draft review and local implementation. That gives us more ways to examine a problem and prepare work for a decision.

Your business knowledge and our judgment remain essential. We are responsible for the recommendations and work we present. The starting point is a conversation about the work your business needs.

## Every client starts with discovery

Discovery is part of every engagement. We need to understand what you sell, who you serve, what customers ask and what your team can actually deliver. We also need the practical details: available materials, account ownership, required access, budget and the person who can confirm decisions.

You do not need a polished strategy document. A few real customer questions, an example of your work and an honest explanation of a recurring problem are useful inputs. A service area or seasonal restriction may change the recommendation more than another round of generated ideas.

We use that conversation to agree the service, scope and next steps. Your knowledge gives the work its substance; technology can help organize and explore it.

## Consider a Google Ads decision

Suppose a repair business wants more inquiries for one service in a limited area. This is an example, not a client result. AI assistance could help organize research, compare possible messages or flag questions in a draft plan.

The important decisions still need people. Which jobs does the owner want? Is the advertised service available? What geography and media budget are authorized? Can someone handle the response? A suggested headline must match those answers before it becomes useful.

A calculation can also compare assumed costs and conversion rates. It cannot turn assumptions into actual demand or a promised customer. We need to keep estimates, observed results and unanswered questions distinct.

## Consider a change to a website

A page might need a clearer service explanation and a working route to schedule a conversation. AI-assisted implementation can help prepare a local version to inspect. Draft review can surface inconsistent wording or a missing step.

Preparing that version is different from confirming that a real booking destination works. Someone still has to inspect the route, check it against the agreed request and decide whether it is ready to publish. A tool's confident description is not evidence that the action succeeded.

This is a way to understand the division of work, not a promise that every engagement uses the same tools or includes a new booking system. Any implementation needs its own agreed scope.

## Human-written SEO has a specific meaning

Our human-written SEO service includes final article prose written by a real person. Agreed research, organization or quality-check assistance can support that work. An AI-generated article does not become human-written simply because someone edits it.

The authorship process and any permitted assistance are agreed before that service begins. This educational page explains our broader use of technology; it does not replace that service commitment or change its deliverables.

## Keep the work and the decisions clear

Human judgment remains responsible for the recommendation, the relationship and the finished work we present. You choose the scope and budget, authorize relevant access and agree the start. Questions about particular tools, source material or review steps belong in that conversation, rather than being left to assumptions.

Technology is not an additional service on the menu. Each $400 service has its own scope and agreement; media, software and other applicable costs are identified separately. Choose the work you need, then agree how it will be done.
