## Keep the page and the campaign working together

Search engine optimization helps build useful pages around the services, locations and questions that matter to customers. Google Ads can bring those pages in front of people searching for help.

Both depend on a clear offer and a practical contact route. We review them together, along with the information needed to understand the inquiries that follow.

## Local search work you can use

Support can include service and service-area pages, useful answers to buyer questions, project or use-case content, Google Business Profile post copy, and updates informed by search data and competitor gaps.

The content should explain the work the business actually performs. A useful page gives someone enough context to decide whether to call, ask a question or request a quote.

## Google Ads with a defined focus

We can set up or clean up search campaigns, organize keywords and ad groups, write search ads, and review search terms and negative keywords. We also examine the budget, destination page and tracking requirements.

Those details matter when a small budget is being spent on vague or unsuitable searches. The next useful change may be an exclusion, a more specific service focus or a page that better matches the ad.

## Agree on readiness before spending

The landing page, tracking, budget and lead handling should be ready before a campaign begins. Ad spend is separate and approved before launch.

Some businesses may also qualify for Local Services Ads. Availability depends on the business category, location and Google's current screening and verification requirements. We check the fit before making it part of the plan.

## Choose the first practical improvement

If your website is unclear, service and contact information may come first. If a campaign is already running, its search terms and inquiry records can show where targeting or measurement needs attention. The answer depends on what is in place.

Monthly content, where useful, means substantive pages and articles about your services, operating area and customers' decisions. Reporting should explain what changed and what needs attention next.

Send the website, the area you serve and the kind of work you want more of. We can review the current setup and recommend where to begin.
