## The owner needed to know what the plan required

When the owner reviewed the content plan, they asked whether producing monthly videos would become their responsibility. Before approving the work, they wanted to understand the commitment.

We clarified that the planned search content was written articles, which our team would produce. The owner approved the outline and understood which decisions and account steps needed their involvement.

## We took the approved plan into production

Our work included developing the content plan, organizing learning content on the website, handling article production and publication, and setting up the search campaign.

The owner approved the direction, granted access and completed the business account steps that required them. We supplied updates on the published article and campaign refinements so the work and next steps remained visible.

## What the handoff showed

At launch, the owner asked what they needed to do next. There were no outstanding marketing action items for them at that point.

The engagement had a clear division of responsibility, with actual content and campaign work delivered. The owner could see what had been done and where their input was needed.

## Keep the promise specific

This anonymized case describes the completed work and the handoff at launch. It does not imply that the owner never needed to participate again or establish a change in lead volume.

Good support makes participation manageable. An owner should know what they need to decide, what access is required and which work someone else will carry through.
