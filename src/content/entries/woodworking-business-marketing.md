## Show what the shop is equipped to do

Someone considering custom work needs to understand the project types you take on, the area you serve and whether your work matches what they have in mind.

That calls for clear service descriptions and useful examples. We help organize the website around the work you want to win, whether that is furniture, cabinets, built-ins, millwork or another defined specialty.

## Put project evidence to work

Approved photographs and project details can support service pages, project stories, Google Business Profile updates and social posts. They give prospective customers something specific to assess before asking for a quote.

We can help turn those materials into useful content, explain the service and identify the next step. Customer names, private project details and photographs should be used only with appropriate permission.

The shop supplies the facts and source material. The content plan should make clear which writing, publishing and campaign work is being handled for you.

## Focus search on the services you want

Support can include service pages by project type, relevant service-area pages, Google Business Profile recommendations and search advertising organized around specific services.

For paid search, we review search terms, exclusions, ad copy and the page that receives the visitor. The owner's notes about inquiry quality help distinguish a useful prospect from a click that asks for work the shop does not want.

There is no need to advertise every possible service. The campaign should reflect what is suitable to deliver and worth attracting.

## Give the customer a useful starting point

A prospective customer should be able to see what you make, understand the kind of project that fits and find a straightforward way to contact the shop.

Depending on the service, useful content might explain project types, materials, the information needed for an initial discussion or how to prepare for a quote request. The details should reflect your actual process.

## Begin with the current work and setup

Bring your website, Google profile, approved project photos and the services you want to prioritize. If advertising is already running, we can review it alongside what the inquiries have been like.

From there, we can define the first useful page, content plan or campaign improvement. Ad budget and launch timing are agreed separately before spend begins.
