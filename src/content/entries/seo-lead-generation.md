## Give visibility somewhere useful to go

A page needs to explain the service well enough for a prospective customer to choose a next step. The form, phone link or other contact route then needs to connect with the business that will respond.

We look across that path so search work, website content and lead handling support the same objective.

## What we work on

**Search visibility and structure.** Review how the site describes its services, how pages connect and where useful content is missing.

**Pages and content.** Develop service pages, landing-page plans and content around the questions prospective customers need answered.

**Search fundamentals.** Improve relevant titles, descriptions, internal links and structured data so the site's content is clearly described.

**Measurement and response.** Set up conversion measurement and recommend improvements to inquiry capture and follow-up.

**Campaign preparation.** Prepare Google Ads work when paid search fits the objective and the business is ready to handle the response.

## What the work can include

A scoped engagement can produce an SEO opportunity map, landing-page plan, metadata updates, content roadmap, conversion tracking setup or lead generation campaign brief.

We prioritize those outputs around the problem. A business with unclear service pages needs different work from one with a useful website and unreliable inquiry measurement.

## Make the outcome visible

Reporting should distinguish visits and contact clicks from completed inquiries. Where the business can provide the information, it should also help explain whether those inquiries fit the offer and moved toward a useful conversation.

That feedback informs the next content, page or campaign change. Search visibility alone does not tell the whole story.

## Before paid activity begins

The landing page, contact route, tracking and lead handling need to be ready for the campaign. Ad spend is separate from service work, with budget and launch approval agreed in advance.

Bring your website, the services you want to promote and what you currently know about the inquiries you receive. We can identify where the path needs attention first.
